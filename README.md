### Concept Extension — Return-Aware Experimentation

In industry experimentation, companies like Netflix do not treat p < 0.05 as a universal decision rule. Instead, they evaluate experiments based on expected business impact relative to risk — a small but reliable gain may ship, while a statistically significant but negligible change may not. This highlights that decision thresholds are economic parameters, not scientific constants. Statistical significance informs evidence; business value determines action.
